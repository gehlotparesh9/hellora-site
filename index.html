Ground rules

Built to be upfront with your customers.

Callers know it's an AI

Every call opens by saying it is a virtual assistant and that the call may be recorded.

No advice, no guessing

It does not give medical, legal or financial advice, and it does not invent prices or answers. When it is unsure it takes a message.

Your number stays yours

You forward calls to us. Turn forwarding off and you are back to how things were.

Questions

What owners ask first.

What happens when it can't answer something?

It says so, takes the caller's name, number and reason, and sends the message to your team straight away. It does not make something up.

Does it work with my phone system?

If your business line can forward calls, it works. That covers most landlines, VoIP services and mobile numbers.

Can it connect to my POS, CRM or booking software?

Calendar booking works from day one. Other systems such as a POS or CRM depend on what they allow, so we check each one with you before promising it.

What does it cost?

Standard is C$149 a month (up to 300 call minutes), Pro is C$349 a month (up to 1,000 minutes) and Ultimate is C$699 a month with unlimited minutes, subject to fair use. Extra minutes on Standard and Pro are C$0.50 each. Setup is a one-time C$299 for our first 8 customers, then C$499. Prices are in Canadian dollars and do not include tax; applicable taxes (HST or GST) are added at checkout. We confirm everything in writing before you commit.

Is it right for a healthcare practice?

For booking and general questions, yes. We keep health details off the call: it collects a name, phone number and appointment type, and it sends anything clinical to your staff.

What happens when someone fills in my contact form?

With their consent, Hellora can phone them back, ask a few questions about what they need and book a time with your team. Each call is approved by you first, and the caller can say stop at any time.

I don't have a website. Can you set up a contact form for me?

Yes. We can build a simple enquiry page for your business on its own hellora.ca address, so people can reach you and Hellora can follow up. Ask us for a live demo.

Claims and availability

Hellora is an early-stage service. This page describes what it does today and what is planned, so you know what to expect.

Working today in our proofs of concept

  • Answers calls with an AI voice and tells callers it is an AI assistant.
  • Books appointments into a connected calendar while the caller is on the line.
  • Takes pickup orders by voice, repeats them back and says the total with tax.
  • Alerts the business when a booking or order is made, by email and chat message.
  • Emails a summary of each call.
  • Follows up web enquiries by phone: after you approve each call, it phones the person who filled in a contact form, asks a few questions and books a call with your team.
  • A client dashboard: sign in with an emailed link to see your calls, summaries, transcripts and recordings, and your web enquiries.
  • Learns the business (hours, services, FAQs) from its website.

Planned not available yet

  • Handoff to a person: passing a call to your staff live.
  • Text-message (SMS) alerts and confirmations.
  • Automatic enquiry calls without approval, and per-business contact pages for every client. Today the contact pages are built by us and each call is approved first.
  • Direct link to your point-of-sale or online store for orders.
  • Additional languages beyond English.

Industries

  • Dental and clinic booking and pizza ordering are the two we have built and tested.
  • Car dealerships, real estate, home services, salons and others describe what we plan to build for each. We will confirm what is ready before you start.

Timing and pricing

  • "Live in days, not months" is our goal for a typical setup, not a guarantee. We confirm a timeline with you first.
  • Pricing is confirmed in writing before you commit.

Privacy and recording

  • Callers are told the call may be recorded and that they are speaking with an AI assistant.
  • For healthcare, the assistant collects only a name, phone number and appointment type. It does not give medical advice.
