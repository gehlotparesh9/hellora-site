What Hellora does for you

  • Answers every call in a natural voice, any time of day, including nights, weekends and holidays.
  • Books appointments straight into your calendar.
  • Takes messages and sends you a clear summary of each call.
  • Passes urgent calls to a person when you want it to.
  • Follows up on enquiries and sends reminders.

Getting started in 4 steps

  1. Tell us about your business: the online form (about 5 minutes). After you pay, we email you from hello@hellora.ca. Tap Tell us about your business in that email. It opens a private form on hellora.ca, linked to your payment. It asks for your business name and your name, business phone, website, type of business and address, opening hours, services, booking rules (length, notice, cancellations), who to pass urgent calls to and when, your calendar (Google, Outlook or none), the email for your call reports, your phone provider, your most common questions and answers, and anything else we should know. Tick the confirmation box and send. We email you a confirmation straight away. Lost the email? Write to hello@hellora.ca. Something changes later? Reply to any Hellora email.
  2. We build your receptionist and email you a test number. When it is ready, we email you from hello@hellora.ca with your test phone number.
  3. Call it and tell us what to change. Phone the test number several times: ask about prices and hours, book a visit, ask for a person. Reply to our email with anything you would like changed.
  4. Go live. When you are happy, forward your business calls to your Hellora number. We help you with this step. See the provider guide below.

Emails from Hellora: how to know they are genuine

Every email we send you comes from one address: hello@hellora.ca. You may see the name Hellora or Hellora Calls next to it. It covers your welcome email, the business form link, your test number, call summaries, appointment confirmations and reports. If you reply, your reply reaches us.

  • The address ends in @hellora.ca, with no extra words, numbers or letters.
  • Links go to hellora.ca, app.hellora.ca or Stripe's billing page (billing.stripe.com).
  • Our emails carry the Hellora logo, the orange line and the signature "Hellora | AI Phone Receptionist".

We will never ask for a password, a card number or a bank transfer by email. Payments are only made through the secure Stripe page. If an email looks odd, do not click it: forward it to hello@hellora.ca and we will check.

Before you became a customer you may have received a message from Paresh at paresh@gethellora.ca. Once you are a customer, everything comes from hello@hellora.ca. Your dashboard at app.hellora.ca always shows the real status of your calls.

Your dashboard and call summaries

Sign in at app.hellora.ca with the email you used when you paid. We send a one-time sign-in link to that address, so there is no password to remember. You can see every call with its summary and transcript, who booked and when, and which calls need a follow up.

After every call, a summary is emailed to the report address you gave in the form. This is on for every customer and is all you need. If a caller asked for a person and nobody could be reached, the summary is marked NEEDS A HUMAN, so call them back.

Optional: instant alerts on Telegram

Telegram is a free messaging app. Hellora can send you a short message each time a call finishes or an appointment is booked. You do not need it. Email already tells you everything.

Part A: install Telegram (skip if you already have it)

  1. On your phone, open the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android).
  2. Search for Telegram Messenger and tap Get or Install. Open it.
  3. Tap Start Messaging, enter your mobile number and tap the arrow.
  4. Telegram sends you a login code (by text or inside the app). Type it in, then enter your name.

Part B: connect Hellora

  1. On the same phone, open the email titled "We have your details. Thank you" from hello@hellora.ca. We send it right after you submit your business form. Scroll down to Optional: instant alerts on Telegram.
  2. Tap the orange Connect Telegram button.
  3. If your phone asks which app to use, choose Telegram. If a web page opens first, tap Open in Telegram or Send message.
  4. A chat with Hellora Alerts (@HelloraAlertsBot) opens. Tap the START button at the bottom of the screen.
  5. Within a few seconds you receive a message saying Telegram is connected. That is all.

What you will receive

  • A short note when a call finishes: "Call finished", "Appointment booked" or "Call passed to a person", with the caller's number, how long they spoke and a summary.
  • An urgent note starting NEEDS A HUMAN when a caller asked for a person and nobody could be reached. Call them back.
  • Your usual email summaries carry on as normal.

On a computer

Install Telegram Desktop from telegram.org, or open web.telegram.org, and log in with the same mobile number. The Hellora Alerts chat appears there too.

Not working?

  • The button opened a web page and nothing happened: tap Open in Telegram. If you do not have Telegram yet, install it first, then tap the button in the email again.
  • Cannot find the email: check your spam folder for "We have your details", or write to hello@hellora.ca and we will send you a new connect link.
  • No confirmation message: check that you pressed START in the Hellora Alerts chat, then write to hello@hellora.ca and we will connect it for you.
  • No sound or popups: open your phone's Settings, then Notifications, then Telegram, and turn notifications on.

Turning it off

Open the Hellora Alerts chat, tap the name at the top and choose Mute or Delete chat. Your emails are not affected.

Passing calls to a person, changing details, getting help

Passing a call to a person. You choose when Hellora hands a call over (for example urgent problems, or a caller who asks for a person) and which number it should ring. If nobody answers, Hellora takes a message and marks the call as needing a follow up.

Changing your information. Hours changed? New service? Reply to any Hellora email, or write to hello@hellora.ca, and tell us what to update.

Callers and recording. Callers should know they are speaking with an AI assistant, and rules on recording calls vary by province. We help you set up the greeting and notices that fit your business. See our Terms.

Billing. You receive a receipt from Stripe for every payment. To update your card, see invoices or cancel, use the billing portal link in your plan email. You can cancel any time (refunds and cancellation).

Need help? Email hello@hellora.ca, or reply to any email from us. Please include your business name and, if it is about a call, the caller's number and roughly when it happened.
