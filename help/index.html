Getting started with Hellora

A short guide for new customers. Download the PDF version.

What Hellora does for you

  • Answers every call in a natural voice, any time of day, including nights, weekends and holidays.
  • Books appointments straight into your calendar.
  • Takes messages and sends you a clear summary of each call.
  • Passes urgent calls to a person when you want it to.
  • Follows up on enquiries and sends reminders.

Getting started in 4 steps

  1. Tell us about your business. After your payment you receive an email with a short form (about five minutes): your hours, services, common questions, how you take bookings, and when to pass a call to a person.
  2. We build your receptionist. We set it up from your answers and send you a test phone number.
  3. Call it and tell us what to change. Phone the test number a few times. Ask about prices, book a visit, ask for a person. Reply to our email with anything you would like changed.
  4. Go live. When you are happy, forward your business calls to your Hellora number. We help you with this step.

Forwarding your business calls

Call forwarding sends calls from your existing business number to your Hellora number. You keep your number. We recommend starting with forward when unanswered or busy: your phone rings first, and if nobody picks up, Hellora answers. You can also forward all calls, which suits after hours and holidays.

  • Tell us who your phone provider is (it is in the form, or just reply to our email).
  • We send you the exact steps for your provider and phone system.
  • Call your business number from another phone. You should hear Hellora answer.

Forwarding codes differ between providers and phone systems. If something does not work the first time, email us and we will walk you through it.

Your dashboard

Sign in at app.hellora.ca with your email. We send you a one-time sign-in link, so there is no password to remember. You can see every call with its summary and transcript, and which calls need a follow up.

Call summaries and alerts

After every call you receive a summary by email. That is on for every customer and is all you need.

Optional: if you would like instant alerts on your phone, you can also connect Telegram. Open your Hellora welcome email, tap Connect Telegram, then tap Start in the Telegram app. If you find it fiddly, skip it. Email already tells you everything.

Passing a call to a person

You choose when Hellora hands a call over (for example urgent problems, or a caller who asks for a person) and which number it should ring. If nobody answers, Hellora takes a message and marks the call as needing a follow up.

Changing your information

Hours changed? New service? Reply to any Hellora email, or write to hello@hellora.ca, and tell us what to update.

Need help?

Email hello@hellora.ca, or just reply to any email from us. Please include your business name and, if it is about a call, the caller's number and roughly when it happened. It helps us fix things faster.
